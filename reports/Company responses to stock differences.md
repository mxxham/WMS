# Prove the difference, let Shell post

When a warehouse's stock disagrees with the stock owner's SAP book, the named companies that leave a public trace all do the same thing. The 3PL's inventory-control desk researches the difference and assigns a cause and a disposition. The owner's stock-accounting team then posts only the final, explained residual, inside a tolerance and an authority matrix. Shell's own job advertisements describe this. Analysts in Shell Business Operations hubs compare GSAP book stock with third-party depot figures daily and at month-end, "investigate and resolve any discrepancies with figures from 3rd party operated depots", and post final differences within tolerance to gain/loss accounts "in accordance to the Manual of Authorities (MoA), in compliance with GSAP and SOX" ([ACCA Careers / Shell](https://jobs.accaglobal.com/job/7480537/analyst-manage-hc-inventory/)). DHL Supply Chain and Kuehne+Nagel staff the 3PL side of the same loop. Indonesian practice adds a formal layer: a recount by a different person, a signed `Berita Acara Selisih Stock Opname` with a witness, and an adjusting journal posted by finance, never a direct edit of the system ([MAS Cargo Express](https://www.mascargoexpress.com/stock-opname-partial-adalah/)). Very few fixes have measured results that are not vendor claims. The best one is an Indonesian distributor whose monthly stock difference fell from **4–7% to 1–2%** after it fixed receiving data entry, putaway placement and posting delay ([Polibatam](https://repository.polibatam.ac.id//bitstreams/8929a65e-8c55-4235-9fd7-273e8a503344/download)). For PT Cipta Krida Bahari (CKB), which holds the physical truth but cannot post in SAP, this means three things. Its leverage is evidence quality and speed. Today's plus/minus pair should be explained as a pair before anyone calls it a gain or a loss. And every correction should go to Shell as a documented, signed request that the next SAP export verifies.

Claims published by a technology vendor about its own product are marked **[vendor claim]**. Statements about what the CKB WMS already does come from the repository and the earlier report *WMS SAP stock discrepancy handling* (same folder). That report covers generic procedure: cut-off, root-cause order, blind recount, netting, SAP movement types, shrink allowance and benchmarks. This report summarises it only where needed. The public record is thin. No named 3PL, no oil major and no Indonesian 3PL publishes its stock-difference SOP, its tolerance or a settlement figure. Those terms sit in private contracts, and the Shell–CKB warehousing agreement is the only authoritative source for them.

## Global 3PLs run a reconciliation desk, not a write-off routine

The clearest view of how large 3PLs handle a book-versus-physical gap comes from their job postings, not their marketing. DHL Supply Chain's Inventory Control Analyst and Coordinator roles list "identifying and resolving variance between WM and customer host system", "reconciliation and data entry into customer's system of record and the WM System", and root-cause analysis of transactions ([DHL careers](https://careers.dhl.com/amer/en/job/DPDHGLOBAL10488863ENAMEREXTERNALTALENTREEF/Inventory-Control-Analyst); [DHL careers](https://careers.dhl.com/eu/pl/job/DPDHGLOBAL10524610PLEUEXTERNALTALENTREEF/Inventory-Control-Coordinator)). DHL's Inventory Control Manager reports on **"inventory accuracy, shrink compliance and variance root-cause trends"** ([DHL careers](https://careers.dhl.com/eu/pl/job/DPDHGLOBAL10538793PLEUEXTERNALTALENTREEF/Inventory-Control-Manager-I)). Kuehne+Nagel states the goal of its contract-logistics inventory specialist as **"100% accuracy in stock reconciliation for each customer account"** ([K+N careers](https://careers.kuehne-nagel.com/job/Contract-Logistics-Inventory-Specialist/8251-en_US)). Its US analyst role finds root causes "so discrepancies can be properly dispositioned", working through named categories: adjustments, batch issues, blocked stock and PO reversals ([K+N careers](https://careers.kuehne-nagel.com/job/Des-Plaines-Inventory-Control-Analyst-Des-Plaines,-IL-IL-60018/6421-en_US)). These come from search summaries of postings, several of which had expired when fetched.

**(Inference)** Read together, these postings describe a five-step loop. A variance is detected. A desk researches the transaction history. It assigns a cause and a disposition. Both systems are brought into line, by the 3PL where the contract lets it key into the client's system, otherwise by the client. Trends are reported against accuracy and shrink KPIs. The client's system is treated as a separate book to reconcile, never overwritten. "Shrink compliance" implies a contractual shrink allowance tracked site by site. CKB sits in the variant where the client posts, which makes the evidence package CKB's main product.

Technology programmes are the only named, quantified 3PL cases, and they report time saved more than accuracy. DHL Supply Chain Malaysia cut cycle-count time **92% (84 h to 6 h)** with Infinium drones and claims "100% inventory count accuracy" across 2,000 locations in 8 hours. This was relayed by the vendor through trade press ([Business Today Malaysia](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/07/16/ai-drone-cuts-92-cycle-count-time-for-dhl-in-malaysia/)) **[vendor claim]**. DSV runs **75 Verity drones** linked to its WMS CargoWrite, scanning moved locations nightly and doing full scans periodically, and says data becomes "virtually error-free" without giving a figure ([DSV](https://www.dsv.com/en/our-solutions/logistics-solutions/contract-logistics/warehousing/warehouse-automation/automated-drones)). Maersk's Miami proof of concept reported **90% fewer staff scanning or searching and 50% less management time correcting errors** ([Maersk Innovation](https://innovation.maersk.com/innovations/verity-autonomous-drones)). GEODIS, through Gather AI, went from four counters doing 800 locations a day, with lost pallets taking 1–2 days to find, to one operator doing 1,200 a day and searches taking minutes. The same vendor page contradicts itself on the size of the time saving (50% in the body, 75% in the summary) ([Gather AI](https://gather.ai/customer-stories/geodis)) **[vendor claim]**. None of these has been independently audited, and none reports variance value recovered.

**(Inference)** The practice worth copying from these programmes is not the drone. It is the rule underneath it: **recount every location that moved today, and attach photo evidence to every exception**, so the client accepts the 3PL's figure without sending its own counter. At 2,970 bins, a phone-based recount of the day's touched pick faces does the same job.

## Shell posts inside a tolerance, and must do so in the same period

Shell publishes no 3PL reconciliation standard. Its public distributor programme, DPQA, covers product quality only ([DPQA brochure](https://www.kellerheartt.com/cdn/shop/files/DPQA_FORM_No_10_QUALIFICATION_TRI-FOLD_BROCHURE.pdf?3989)). Shell's job postings, however, reveal the owner-side process in some detail. Stock-accounting analysts in Business Operations centres such as Kuala Lumpur, Manila and Krakow compare book with third-party figures daily, reconcile at period end, and investigate differences "outside of tolerance" ([ACCA Careers / Shell](https://jobs.accaglobal.com/job/3551742/analyst-manage-hydrocarbon-inventory/)). They post final differences within tolerance to gain/loss under the MoA ([ACCA Careers / Shell](https://jobs.accaglobal.com/job/7480537/analyst-manage-hc-inventory/)). A fetched Krakow posting lists a "stocks reconciliation program daily/weekly/endmonth/bi-annually physical stocks count exercise (cyclecounting and wall-to-wall stocks take)", "justifications for all material stock movements" to finance monthly, and "provisions management" ([karierawfinansach.pl](https://www.karierawfinansach.pl/oferta-pracy/112798,outbound-finished-goods-officer-krakow)). These postings concern hydrocarbons and finished goods. Whether Shell Lubricants Indonesia uses the same team is unconfirmed. TotalEnergies even has a named "Lubricants Stores Supervisor – Reconciliation" role ([TotalEnergies](https://jobs.totalenergies.com/es_ES/careers/JobDetail/ASSISTANT-LUBRICANTS-STORES-SUPERVISOR-RECONCILIATION/71962)).

One SAP constraint shapes the owner's behaviour. MI07 can post a count difference only in the fiscal period in which the count took place, and a back-dated posting uses current stock, not stock at the count date ([SAP KBA 1589511](https://userapps.support.sap.com/sap/support/knowledge/en/1589511)). **(Inference)** Shell's analyst therefore needs CKB's explanation **before month-end close**. An item still unexplained at close becomes harder for Shell to post cleanly, and more likely to turn into a provision or a claim against the 3PL. The speed of CKB's explanation is worth money to Shell.

The best FMCG case shows what an owner finds once it automates the comparison. JK Tech's unnamed FMCG client, with food, home-care and beauty brands, had stock controllers reconciling several 3PL WMSs against its SAP by hand, daily for interfaced sites and weekly for the rest. The root causes were interface failures (IDocs), WMS transactions with no preceding SAP document, and WMS transactions that never triggered SAP. The fix was a rule-based daily report that proposes a likely root cause and resolution path for each difference and emails it to the owner of the reconciliation. The benefits were published only in qualitative terms ([JK Tech](https://jktech.com/success-stories/enabling-business-transformation-by-eliminating-stock-mismatch-reconciliation-increasing-stock-accuracy-and-reducing-financial-risk/)). SAP's knowledge base treats these mismatches as an expected failure mode: unconfirmed goods issues, ghost stock after a UoM change, duplicate postings, and restricted stock hidden by a default filter ([SAP KBA 3724761](https://userapps.support.sap.com/sap/support/knowledge/en/3724761); [SAP KBA 3666118](https://userapps.support.sap.com/sap/support/knowledge/en/3666118)). **(Inference)** For CKB, which exchanges files rather than IDocs, the equivalent categories are an unposted GI or GR, a delivery posted against the wrong SKU or batch, stock still on the inbound location, and blocked versus unrestricted stock. Every difference should be classified into one of these before it is treated as physical.

On liability, generic contract practice is consistent. The owner absorbs shrink up to an allowance. The 3PL is liable above it when the loss happened in its custody and procedures were not followed. Liability is often capped, sometimes at a multiple of monthly fees, and some contracts split unexplained shrink 50/50 ([Racklify](https://racklify.com/encyclopedia/liability-insurance-and-contract-tips-for-managing-lost-inventory-with-3pls/); [Cleverence](https://www.cleverence.com/amp/articles/3pl-business/liability-for-lost-or-damaged-goods-risk-for-3pls-4827/)). No Shell tolerance, valuation basis (cost or selling price) or charge-back rule is public.

## Indonesian practice runs on signed berita acara and finance journals

Indonesian sources describe a stricter document trail than the global ones. MAS Cargo Express's procedure has six steps ([MAS Cargo Express](https://www.mascargoexpress.com/stock-opname-partial-adalah/)):

1. A recount by a **different officer**.
2. A trace through `surat jalan`, `bukti terima` and receipts.
3. A comparison against tiered tolerances. Its examples are 0% for high-value goods, 1% for fast movers and 2% for low-value goods.
4. Approval by a supervisor or warehouse manager.
5. A `berita acara` listing counters, a **witness (`saksi`)**, items, suspected cause, corrective action and the responsible person's signature.
6. A `jurnal penyesuaian` posted by finance under PSAK 202, never a direct edit of the system.

Indonesian accounting guides add a valuation rule (units times `harga pokok`). They also raise the question of whether a difference becomes a company expense or is **charged to the warehouse person in charge** ([Mekari Expense](https://expense.mekari.com/blog/panduan-stock-opname)). Under PSAK 14, normal small losses may sit in cost of goods sold, while abnormal ones go to a separate inventory-loss line ([Binus Accounting](https://accounting.binus.ac.id/2026/05/31/kerugian-persediaan-dalam-psak-14/)). PT Rapi Trans Logistik Indonesia's control phase is a good picture of routine supervision. It runs a daily stock-check sheet (physical, system, difference) verified by the warehouse supervisor, a weekly report reviewed by the logistics manager, and weekly audits ([Candra & Dewantoro 2025](https://jurnal.ukdc.ac.id/index.php/isejou/article/download/784/442/4103)).

Three legal layers sit behind this. Under the Civil Code (KUHPerdata Art. 1706–1714), a warehouse holding someone else's goods (a bailee) owes the same care it gives its own goods. A stricter duty applies only if the contract says so. Without a contract clause, the owner must prove negligence ([Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/wajibkah-penerima-titipan-mengganti-barang-yang-hilang-lt5419979b673ce/)). For tax, destroying damaged stock is deductible only with strong evidence. Tax-office account representatives witness destructions and sign the `berita acara` ([DDTC News](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/daerah/1803985/petugas-pajak-awasi-kegiatan-pemusnahan-barang-persediaan-milik-wp); [pajak.go.id](https://pajak.go.id/en/node/71300)). A consultant notes that a BA signed only by internal staff is easy for auditors to reject, and that an item-level list with photos or video is expected ([Enforcea](https://enforcea.com/Blog/biaya-pemusnahan-persediaan-bisa-dikoreksi-total-jika-dokumen-ini-tidak-ada)) (consultant, unverified). For the environment, expired, spilled or off-spec lubricant and its used packaging are **B3 waste** under PP 22/2021. They go to a licensed handler with delivery documents, and the generator keeps responsibility ([CRPG summary](https://crpg.info/b3-waste-labeling-and-packaging-indonesias-safety-framework-under-pp-22-2021/)). The ministry has sealed an unlicensed used-oil processor ([KemenLH](https://kemenlh.go.id/news/detail/tindak-lanjut-keluhan-masyarakat-menteri-lh-tugaskan-deputi-gakkum-segel-pabrik-pemanfaatan-oli-bekas-ilegal-di-tangerang)). These are secondary summaries of the regulations. Confirm them with Shell's tax and HSSE teams.

**(Inference)** In a 3PL setting, the client's representative becomes the extra signatory on the `berita acara` before the client posts in its own system. A gain or loss on Shell stock is a Shell posting in GSAP, and the tax evidence is Shell's. A damaged carton therefore never just leaves the WMS. It moves to Quarantine and leaves only through a disposal movement backed by Shell's written approval, a licensed B3 manifest and a photographed, witnessed BA. Who counts as the legal B3 "generator", Shell as owner or CKB as site operator, is a contract question that no public source settles. No named Indonesian 3PL (Kamadjaja, Puninar, Dunex, Enseval, CKB Logistics and others), and no Shell or Pertamina lubricant distributor, publishes its procedure or its settlement terms (`klaim selisih`, `potong tagihan`).

## Fixing the cause works; counting and adjusting alone does not

The evidence on what reduces differences is uneven. The table grades each fix by source type.

| Fix | Measured result | Evidence grade |
|---|---|---|
| Receiving SOP, layout/5S, training and system fixes at PT Bintang Lima Imada (Morinaga distributor) | Monthly difference **4–7% → 1–2%**. The receiving-SOP fix had the strongest effect ([Polibatam](https://repository.polibatam.ac.id//bitstreams/8929a65e-8c55-4235-9fd7-273e8a503344/download)) | Local student study, one period |
| Barcode/RFID recording, stricter SOP and withdrawal control at PT Rapi Trans | Differences **−80%**, recording accuracy +92% ([Candra & Dewantoro](https://jurnal.ukdc.ac.id/index.php/isejou/article/download/784/442/4103)) | Local journal, 20 SKUs, own spare parts |
| Manual updates replaced by RF scan-to-confirm (Fisk Industries) | **85% → 98%** accuracy ([casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/rf-smart/case-study/fisks-beauty-secret-for-increasing-inventory-accuracy)) | Vendor claim |
| Barcoded location labels (appliance maker) | Location accuracy "nearing 100%" ([Camcode](https://www.camcode.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/appliance-giant-case-study.pdf)) | Vendor claim |
| Drones (Langham Logistics, Taylor Logistics) | ~97% → >99% accuracy, 8-month ROI ([Gather AI](https://gather.ai/case-studies)) | Vendor claim, figures inconsistent across pages |
| Item-level RFID in retail apparel (Auburn/GS1) | 63% → 95% accuracy ([Retail Dive](https://www.retaildive.com/news/RFID-100-accurate-ROI-Auburn/539468)) | Academic-industry, not cartons |
| Pick-to-light | "Up to 50%" lower error costs, attributed to Fraunhofer ([ekko.io](https://www.ekko.io/en/wissen/pick-to-light-advantages)) | Vendor; original study not traced |
| Higher cycle-count frequency (PT ABC) | 98.83% accuracy over 200 cycles ([IPB](https://journal.ipb.ac.id/index.php/jurnalmpi/article/view/56445/28309)) | Local journal, no control group |

The Bintang Lima Pareto of 131 findings is the most transferable diagnosis. **42%** were data-entry errors on goods in or out, **27%** wrong placement, **18%** delay between the physical move and the system posting, and **14%** goods on hold ([Polibatam](https://repository.polibatam.ac.id//bitstreams/8929a65e-8c55-4235-9fd7-273e8a503344/download)). Theft does not appear. The academic work points the same way. Across ~370,000 retail records, **65%** were inaccurate. The variance between product categories (26.4%) dwarfed the variance between stores (2.7%), and auditing practices reduced inaccuracy ([DeHoratius & Raman 2008](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:inm:ormnsc:v:54:y:2008:i:4:p:627-641)). In one audit, **60%** of stockouts came from goods physically present in the wrong place ([CMR 2001](https://cmr.berkeley.edu/2001/05/43-3-execution-the-missing-link-in-retail-operations/)). Kang and Gershwin show that small uncorrected losses compound into severe stock-outs, and that process methods can treat this without automatic identification ([MIT DSpace](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/36193)).

The documented failure mode is "simply update the system to match the count", which leaves the cause in place ([Plant Services](https://www.plantservices.com/management/supply-chain-management/article/11335568/road-to-reliability-the-road-to-accurate-inventory-cycle-counting-uses-experienced-counters-of-inventory-to-determine-reasons-why-errors-occur-plant-services)). Its two-system variant is adjusting one system without a matching posting in the other, which moves the difference rather than removing it ([SAP Community](https://community.sap.com/t5/supply-chain-management-q-a/adjust-stock-in-ewm-but-not-in-erp/qaq-p/12650517)). No study measures accuracy with versus without root-cause work, so this rests on practitioner consensus and modelling, not a controlled trial.

**(Inference)** CKB already scans rack labels and posts in real time, so the large "manual to scanned" gains are mostly behind it. Its remaining exposure matches the local Pareto. Receiving entry (SKU, batch, expiry, quantity at check), putaway into the wrong bin, mispicks between look-alike SKUs, and the lag between WMS and Shell's postings are what remain. The category-over-site finding argues for counting look-alike SKU families (same brand, different viscosity or pack size) more often than the rest. Drones and RFID do not pay at 2,970 bins of cartons, and they count open pick faces worst.

## What CKB can do, starting with today's plus and minus

**(Inference throughout this section.)** The global, Shell and Indonesian practices converge on one division of labour. CKB proves the physical side and explains the difference. Shell decides and posts. The `berita acara` makes the handover formal. The earlier report's investigation order (cut-off, scope, timing, UoM, partner difference, then count) still applies. What follows adds the company and Indonesian layer.

| # | Action | Who | Why, from the evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Treat today's pair as one case.** Check that both figures are at the same cut-off and include WHS1 plus the inbound location. Match the last few days of SAP GI/GR against WMS shipments and receipts by DO number. Test whether the gap equals a delivery quantity or a UPP multiple. Check whether the two SKUs are look-alikes. If the plus and minus are equal and similar, suspect a mispick, a mis-putaway or a SKU swap on a DO. | CKB inventory control | JK Tech's and SAP's cause lists put interface and posting causes first. Equal and opposite pairs point to wrong placement (27% at Bintang Lima). |
| 2 | **Count both SKUs together, blind, with a second counter**: every bin of each SKU, the pick face first, then the reserve that last fed it, adjacent bays, staging and Quarantine. Read the last pick and putaway tasks on both. Photograph each bin. | CKB | Recount by a different officer (MAS Cargo). Photo evidence is what makes 3PL counts acceptable to clients (GEODIS, Vimaan). |
| 3 | **Fix the WMS only by movement.** A wrong-bin or swapped item found in the count gets a correcting move with no SAP impact. If a DO shipped the wrong SKU, record it as such and do not hide it as a gain/loss pair. | CKB | Adjusting one side without the other only shifts the difference (SAP Community). This follows CKB's movements-only rule. |
| 4 | **Write one `Berita Acara Selisih Stok` per case.** It lists SKU, batch, expiry, cartons in WMS, SAP and counted, the counters and a witness, photos, the cause category, the DO or SAP document involved, the corrective action, and the **SAP correction requested** (for example "post GI for DO x", "transfer SKU A to SKU B", "write off n cartons"). CKB's warehouse head signs it, and Shell's representative countersigns. | CKB prepares, Shell signs | This is the Indonesian standard (MAS Cargo, HashMicro). Shell posts under its MoA only on explained, approved differences. |
| 5 | **Send it before Shell's month-end close** and track it until the next SAP export shows the posting. An unexplained item at close is the one that becomes a provision or a claim. | CKB | MI07 same-period rule (SAP KBA 1589511). |
| 6 | **Keep a reason-coded register** with Shell-relevant codes: unposted GI/GR, wrong SKU or batch on a document, inbound-location timing, blocked/Quarantine status, UoM, receiving error, putaway error, mispick, damage, unknown. Report a monthly Pareto and the unknown share. | CKB | DHL tracks "variance root-cause trends". K+N's named categories. Fix the top cause, not the count. |
| 7 | **Target the causes the Pareto shows.** Scan-confirm SKU and batch at receipt and putaway. Add a check-digit confirm at the pick face for look-alike families. Count the day's touched pick faces nightly, and count look-alike families at A-class frequency. | CKB | Bintang Lima: receiving SOP had the biggest effect. DSV's nightly moved-location scan. DeHoratius & Raman: target by product category. |
| 8 | **Handle damage as a separate stream.** Damaged stock goes to Quarantine with a reason. Report it to Shell. Dispose of it only on Shell's written approval through a licensed B3 handler, with manifest, photos and a BA signed by Shell (and a tax-office witness if Shell needs the deduction). | CKB executes, Shell approves | PP 22/2021. Tax evidence practice. Owners approve disposal (Krakow posting). |
| 9 | **Ask Shell for the contract terms** that no public source gives: the reconciliation cadence, the tolerance and its base (units or value, monthly or annual), the valuation basis for a charge-back, who signs a BA on Shell's side, who is the B3 generator, and what Z31/Z55 mean. | CKB management | Every source says these sit in the private contract. Without a clause, bailment law puts the burden of proving negligence on the owner. |
| 10 | **Agree a joint wall-to-wall stocktake** once or twice a year, signed by both, as the base for shrink against the allowance. The daily comparison measures posting errors, not shrink. | CKB and Shell | Shell's own posting names bi-annual wall-to-wall counts. |

What the WMS already supports, from the repository: blind counts and different-person recounts (`0019`, `0021`), count-every-bin-of-SKU tasks, an approval threshold of 20 cartons with four-eyes (`0018`), the SAP comparison at a 00:00 WIB cut-off with automatic GI matching (`0111`–`0113`), and Buku stok notes per SKU (`0116`). What is missing for this list is a paired-SKU count action, a register with states, aging and the requested SAP correction, a printable `berita acara` per case, and verification of each request against the next upload.

## Conclusion

The public record changes the question CKB should ask. The question is not how other warehouses settle a plus and a minus. It is how fast and how convincingly the warehouse can hand the stock owner an explained difference. Shell's analysts are measured on posting explained differences inside tolerance before close. CKB's contract exposure grows with every item that reaches close unexplained. A paired, photographed, countersigned case file is therefore both the corrective action and CKB's main defence against a charge-back. On prevention, the local evidence is clear. Receiving and putaway discipline and posting lag, not theft or the lack of drones, drove differences at comparable Indonesian sites. The cheapest measurable gain for CKB is to stop the error at entry and to count where the errors concentrate.

The biggest unknowns cannot be found by research. They are in the Shell–CKB agreement and in Shell's GSAP configuration: the tolerance and its base, the valuation and charge-back rule, the meaning of Z31/Z55, and who signs for disposal and B3. Until those are known, CKB should record each requested correction in plain words rather than as movement types. Three months of reason-coded cases will show whether this site's differences are mostly book timing or mostly floor errors, a split no published source measures for a lubricant 3PL.
